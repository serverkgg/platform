## How pricing works

All prices are in Saudi Riyal. Your server is paid monthly: every payment extends it one month from its current expiry date — so extending early never costs you days.

All your orders and receipts live on the [orders page](/dashboard/orders).

## When does your server expire?

Every server shows its expiry date in its panel and on the [billing page](/dashboard/billing). A clear warning appears in the panel days before expiry.

## Extending

From the billing page or the warning itself, hit **Extend** and pay — the extension is instant, needs no restart, and your server is never touched.

> [!warning] A server that expires without extension stops running, and after a grace period its data is permanently deleted. Extend before expiry — a deleted server cannot be recovered.

## Refunds

The full payment and refund policy lives on [its page](/legal/refunds). For any special case, write to support@serverk.gg and we will help.
