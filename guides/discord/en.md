## Join the community

The Serverk Discord is where you find players like you, ask questions, and help others — the [invite link](https://discord.gg/gwGT2Mqd6A) is always in the footer of any page.

## Link your account

Linking your Serverk account with Discord lets you follow your servers from there directly:

1. In Discord, use the link command and you will receive a private link
2. The link opens the claim page on Serverk — sign in if you are not already
3. Confirm the link, and you are done

Once linked you can see your servers' status, players online, and important alerts — without opening the platform.

> [!note] Linking only lets Discord display your servers' status — nobody can control your server or your account from Discord.

## Why link?

- Know your server came back up after an update while you sit in the chat channel
- Your group sees who is online before joining
- Expiry reminders reach you in a place you already check every day
