From a new account to a running server with its own address, step by step.

## Before you start

- An email address you can open, and a username and password you choose when you sign up.
- To know your ping to each region before you pay, follow [Check your ping before you buy](/guides/before-you-buy).

## Steps

### 1. Create your account

Sign up from the [sign-up page](/sign-up) with your email, a username and a password, then press **Create account**. Your account is ready right away.

![The sign-up form with the email, username and password fields and the Create account button.](guides/getting-started/sign-up.webp)

### 2. Pick your game

Open the [New server](/games/create) page and press your game's card under **Pick a game**. Every card shows the RAM we recommend for that game.

![The game cards under Pick a game, each showing its recommended RAM.](guides/getting-started/pick-game.webp)

### 3. Pick a region

Under **Pick a region**, we measure the ping from your device to each region live. The closer the datacenter is to you and your friends, the lower your ping.

![The Pick a region section with the measured ping to each region.](guides/getting-started/pick-region.webp "The ping in the picture is from our test device. Yours shows on your screen.")

### 4. Pick a plan

Under **Pick a plan**, the plan that fits your game carries the **Best fit** badge. Prices show in the currency you pick under **Country & currency** at the top of the page.

![The Pick a plan section with the best-fit plan for the game selected.](guides/getting-started/pick-plan.webp "We hid the prices in the picture, because they depend on your country and currency.")

> [!note] Not sure which plan? Start with the **Best fit** one. You can move to a bigger plan later, and your world and settings stay as they are.

### 5. Pay

Check **Your order**, press **Continue**, then pay. After you pay, we start setting up your server right away, and you can follow its progress live on the order page.

### 6. Finish the setup page

Once your server is ready, the panel opens on the **Setup** page: choose your game's basic settings and confirm them. Every optional step has a **Skip this step** button.

![The Setup page with its steps and the game's basic settings below them.](guides/getting-started/setup.webp)

### 7. Share your address

After setup, your server is ready with its own address, on the **Overview** tab. Copy it with the copy button next to it and send it to your friends.

![The Overview tab with the server address and its copy button.](guides/getting-started/server-address.webp)
