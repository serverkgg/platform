## Create your account

Sign up from the [sign-up page](/sign-up). All you need is an email, a username and a password. Your account is ready instantly, with no complicated activation.

## Pick your game and plan

From the [create a server](/create) page:

1. **Pick a game**: every game states its requirements clearly, the minimum and recommended RAM
2. **Pick a plan**: prices are in Saudi riyals, and the recommended plan is highlighted for each game
3. **Pick a region**: the closer the datacenter is to you and your friends, the lower your ping

> [!note] Not sure which plan? Start with the recommended one. Upgrading later is easy, and you never lose your world or settings.

## Pay and set up your server

After you pay, your machine starts provisioning right away, and you can follow its progress live on the order page. Once it's ready, the panel opens on a short setup page: choose your game's basic settings and confirm, and the install starts. When it finishes, your server is ready with its own address.
