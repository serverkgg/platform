## Create your account

Sign up from the [registration page](/sign-up) — all you need is an email, a username and a password. Accounts are instant, no complicated activation.

## Pick your game and plan

From the [create a server](/create) page:

1. **Pick the game** — every game states its requirements clearly: minimum and recommended RAM
2. **Pick the plan** — prices are in Saudi Riyal, and the recommended plan is highlighted per game
3. **Pick the region** — the datacenter closest to you and your friends means the lowest ping

> [!note] Not sure about the plan? Start with the recommended one. Upgrading later is easy and never costs you your world or settings.

## Pay and set up your server

After payment your machine starts provisioning right away, and you follow its progress live on the order page. Once it is ready the panel opens on a short setup page — you choose your game's basic settings and confirm — and the install runs. When it finishes your server is ready with its own address.
